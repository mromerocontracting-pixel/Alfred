## Method
- **Scale check (A-5, 1/4" = 1'-0", 0.05556 ft/pt):** vector lengths checked against printed 19'-4", 13'-5½", and 14'-4". All three agree within 0.2%.
- **A-2 (1"=10') and S-300 (1/4"=1'):** used to confirm the eave rectangle, hips/ridge, and monitor size. Final dimensions come from printed numbers wherever they exist.
- The plans say "dimensions shall be read and never scaled." Every scaled value is either confirmed by a printed dimension or marked provisional.
