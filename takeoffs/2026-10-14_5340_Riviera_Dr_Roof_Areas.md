## Method
- **Scale check (A-5, 1/4" = 1'-0", 0.05556 ft/pt):** vector lengths checked against printed 19'-4", 13'-5½", and 14'-4". All three agree within 0.2%.
- **Site plan (A-2, 1" = 10'-0"):** used only to confirm the eave rectangle, hip/ridge geometry, and monitor location. All final dimensions come from printed numbers where they exist.
- The plans say "dimensions shall be read and never scaled." Every scaled value above is either confirmed by a printed dimension or marked provisional.
