### 4. Conflicts / questions to resolve
1. **LWIC vs. tapered board.** Detail 4 calls out "New L.W.I.C. (refer to roof plans for scope)". Details 2–3 call for rigid tapered insulation, and the perimeter note mixes LWIC and board. The plan doesn't say which is used where.
2. **Overflow scuppers.** The details say "see plan for overflow scupper sizes", but the plan only shows 3 "scupper (typ.)". Count and size of overflows is unknown.
3. **Base flashing height:** 6" min. (details 2–3) vs. 8" min. (detail 4). Use 8".
4. **Eyebrow north depth:** not dimensioned (≈ 5'-2" scaled).
5. **Eyebrow waterproofing scope:** the plan doesn't show the eyebrow roofed, but detail 3 shows a new torch cap system on it.
