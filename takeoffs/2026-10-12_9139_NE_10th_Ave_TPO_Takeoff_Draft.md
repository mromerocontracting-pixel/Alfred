## 6. Approval gate (HVHZ) – read before pricing

- **Product approval:** the current GAF approval for this system is [Miami-Dade NOA 25-0707.01](https://www.miamidade.gov/building/library/productcontrol/noa/25070701.pdf), "EverGuard TPO Single Ply Roofing Systems over Wood Decks." It expires 09/22/2030 and its maximum design pressure is -120 psf.
- **Matching systems:** System Types **E(3)** and **E(4)** are the non-insulated systems with the membrane mechanically attached directly to plywood ([NOA 25-0707.01](https://www.miamidade.gov/building/library/productcontrol/noa/25070701.pdf)).
  - E(3): Drill-Tec #14 fasteners, 3", with 2" Double Barbed XHD or 2-3/8" Barbed XHD plates. 12" o.c. gives -60 psf; 6" o.c. gives -67.5 psf.
  - E(4): Drill-Tec #14 fasteners, 1.75", with 2-3/4" Barbed SXHD plates at 12" o.c., good to -75 psf.
  - Both systems: rows 48" o.c., 6" laps, 1.5" welds.
- **The mismatch:** E(3) and E(4) are written for 15/32" plywood over **wood** trusses, with fasteners driven **into the trusses with at least 1" of embedment** ([NOA 25-0707.01](https://www.miamidade.gov/building/library/productcontrol/noa/25070701.pdf)). This house has **steel** trusses. Before this assembly can be called permit-ready, we need a GAF technical letter or an engineer's RAS 117 / TAS 105 fastening design.
- **ZIP System is not plywood.** If the deck really is ZIP System (an OSB panel), it does not match the plywood deck in E(3) and E(4). Confirm 5/8" CDX plywood per S-06.
- **Perimeters and corners:** the NOA's General Limitation #7 requires added fastening at perimeters and corners, calculated per RAS 117 / RAS 137 and signed and sealed. General Limitation #8 requires nailers, metal edges and flashing terminations to meet RAS 111 ([NOA 25-0707.01](https://www.miamidade.gov/building/library/productcontrol/noa/25070701.pdf)).
- **Design pressures:** the roof's Zone 1, 2 and 3 pressures are not on these sheets. They must be no higher than -75 psf for E(4), or the system changes.
- **Fire rating:** the NOA does not cover fire classification. It lists VersaShield / VersaShield Solo slip sheets or FireOut coating as optional fire barriers ([NOA 25-0707.01](https://www.miamidade.gov/building/library/productcontrol/noa/25070701.pdf)). Confirm whether a Class A listing over plywood requires one.
