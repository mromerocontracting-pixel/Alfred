## Project Overview
The building has cantilevered concrete slab eyebrows (overhangs) at [locations, e.g. north and west elevations]. These slabs are exposed to the weather and are not covered by the main roofing scope. This proposal covers a Tremco liquid-applied waterproofing system over the top surface of each eyebrow, turned up at the wall and terminated at the slab edge. The system protects the slab and the wall below from water intrusion.

**System:** Tremco [AlphaGuard MT, a fluid-applied, reinforced, moisture-triggered membrane, or the Tremco system named in the specs], applied over Tremco primer, with polyester reinforcing fabric at all transitions, cracks, and terminations, and a [color] UV-stable top coat.

## Scope of Work
1. Mobilize the crew and stage materials. Protect the windows, doors, walls, and finished surfaces below and next to the eyebrows.
2. Prepare the concrete surface by grinding or mechanical abrasion and cleaning to remove laitance, curing compounds, dirt, and loose material, per Tremco's surface-prep requirements. Moisture- and adhesion-test the slab before application.
3. Rout and fill cracks wider than 1/16" and seal cold joints with Tremco-approved sealant, then pre-strip them with fabric-reinforced base coat.
4. Prime all concrete surfaces with Tremco primer at the manufacturer's rate.
5. Detail the wall-to-slab transition with reinforced base coat turned up the wall [6"/8"], with a sealant cant where required. Terminate under [the stucco/existing reglet, or a new termination bar with sealant].
6. Detail the slab edge with reinforced base coat wrapped over the edge to the drip line [or onto new drip edge metal, if selected below].
7. Apply the reinforced base coat over the full eyebrow surface at Tremco's specified thickness, with fabric fully embedded.
8. Apply the top coat at Tremco's specified mils for a uniform, UV-resistant finish. Verify wet-film thickness throughout.
9. Clean up and haul away debris. Do a final walkthrough with the Owner.
10. Provide the product data sheets and the workmanship warranty letter at close-out.
