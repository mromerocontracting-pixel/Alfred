## Project Overview
The existing low-slope TPO roof ends at a perimeter roof edge that runs right next to a neighboring building's exterior wall. An open void, about 6"–8" wide, separates the two structures. Wind-driven rain enters this void at the roof-to-wall interface. This proposal covers furnishing and installing a new manufactured TPO expansion joint system along the full length of this condition. The joint closes the void and stops water intrusion while letting the two structures move independently.

## Scope of Work
1. Mobilize crew and stage materials at the roof area. Protect adjacent finished surfaces, AC equipment, and rooftop appurtenances for the duration of the work.
2. Inspect and prepare the existing 60-mil TPO membrane and roof edge along the full 66 LF span next to the neighboring wall. Clean and prime the substrate as required for adhesion and welding.
3. Furnish and install a new manufactured TPO-compatible expansion joint cover system (GAF EverGuard Extreme TPO Expansion Joint or equal, sized for the 6"–8" void) for the full 66 LF.
   - Roof-side flange: heat-welded to the existing TPO membrane per the manufacturer's installation requirements, forming a continuous, monolithic weld seam.
   - Wall-side flange: mechanically fastened to the adjacent building's wall with a termination bar and corrosion-resistant masonry fasteners at the manufacturer's spacing.
4. Apply sealant at the roof-side weld termination and at the wall-side termination bar, continuous for the full 66 LF, so each attachment point has a fully sealed, redundant weatherproof detail.
5. Fabricate and install a mitered/formed corner transition piece at the direction change identified in the field, keeping the seal continuous through the corner.
6. Field-test welded seams and terminations for continuity. Correct any deficiencies before close-out.
7. Remove all debris, fasteners, and packaging from the work. Magnetic-sweep the work area and leave the roof and adjacent areas broom-clean.
8. Provide close-out documentation, including the product data sheet and workmanship warranty.
