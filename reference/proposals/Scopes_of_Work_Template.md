## Scope #1 — Front Porch Soffit & Fascia: Complete Removal
Demolition only. No new material unless separately authorized.
1. Mobilize labor, equipment, and disposal/dumpster service for the front porch area.
2. Remove existing soffit panels (wood, vinyl, or aluminum) at the front porch overhang.
3. Remove existing fascia boards and any associated trim, J-channel, drip edge, or molding at the front porch.
4. Remove materials carefully to avoid damaging porch columns, ceiling, decking, siding, and adjacent finishes.
5. Inspect exposed rafter tails, sub-fascia, and roof sheathing for rot, water damage, or structural deficiency. Photo-document findings for owner review.
6. Haul away and legally dispose of all removed material off-site.
7. Leave the work area broom-clean and free of debris, nails, and fasteners.

**Exclusions:** new soffit/fascia/trim; structural framing/decking repair (change order); gutters, painting, siding repair.

## Scope #2 — Soffit & Fascia: Complete Removal and Redesign
1. Remove and dispose of all existing soffit and fascia in the designated area(s).
2. Inspect rafter tails, sub-fascia, and sheathing. Repair or replace deteriorated framing/decking as needed (priced separately or T&M).
3. Finalize the redesigned profile with the owner: material (wood/aluminum/vinyl), fascia dimension, vented vs. solid soffit.
4. Install new sub-fascia where required to set the new fascia line.
5. Install new fascia boards per the approved design.
6. Install new soffit panels with continuous ventilation for attic airflow.
7. Install new J-channel, F-channel, and trim.
8. Install/replace drip edge and flashing at the roof-to-fascia transition.
9. Caulk and seal all joints, seams, and penetrations.
10. Prime and paint/finish new material per the selected color, if applicable.
11. Clean up and haul away debris.

**Exclusions:** roofing beyond the flashing tie-in; gutters unless listed; framing repairs beyond the soffit/fascia line (change order).

## Scope #3 — Fascia & Flat Roof Removal with 1/4" Slope for Positive Drainage
1. Remove and dispose of existing fascia along the flat roof area.
2. Remove existing membrane, insulation, and decking (as required) down to the structural deck.
3. Inspect the structural deck and framing; repair as needed (priced separately or T&M).
4. Install a tapered insulation system (or sleeper/shim framing) for a minimum 1/4" per foot slope to drains, scuppers, or leaders.
5. Install new roof decking over the tapered system, as required.
6. Install a new membrane system (TPO, EPDM, or mod-bit, to be specified) per the manufacturer, including seams, penetrations, and perimeter flashing.
7. Install new fascia to match the new roof edge height/line.
8. Install new edge metal, drip edge, and termination bar along the perimeter.
9. Verify and tie in roof drains, scuppers, and overflow provisions.
10. Water-test to confirm positive drainage with no ponding before the final walkthrough.
11. Clean up and haul away debris.

**Exclusions:** interior water damage from pre-existing conditions; gutters/downspouts unless listed; relocation of rooftop equipment unless listed.
