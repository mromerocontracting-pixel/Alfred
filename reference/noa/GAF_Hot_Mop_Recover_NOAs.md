## Rules that apply to every recover system in both NOAs
- **Recover System Limitation #2:** install per the applicable sections of **FBC 1521**. Uplift of assemblies **bonded** to the existing roof is verified per **1521.10**. Uplift of assemblies **mechanically attached through** the existing roof is verified per **1521.11**.
- Insulation set in hot asphalt: **4' × 4' panels max** (BUR NOA, General Limitation #3).
- Insulation fasteners need a field TAS 105 value of **275 lbf minimum**, or insulation attachment is not acceptable (BUR NOA, GL #5).
- Perimeter and corner pressures: where a system cites GL #7, enhanced fastening must be calculated per RAS 117 and sealed by a FL PE, RA or Registered Roof Consultant. Where it cites GL #9, the listed pressure is the maximum for **all zones** and no extrapolation is allowed.
- Nailers, edge metal and flashing terminations per **RAS 111**.

## 25-1017.03 (BUR): insulation hot-mopped directly to the existing roof
Only System Types A(1) and A(2) bond the insulation straight to the existing BUR or mod-bit. Both are on a **structural concrete deck only** and rated **-150 psf** (GL #9, all zones).

| System | Deck | Insulation (adhered to existing) | Base | Plies | Cap | MDP |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A(1) | Structural concrete | EnergyGuard Polyiso or **EnergyGuard Tapered Polyiso**, min 0.5"; optional top board: Structodek HD fiberboard min 0.5", or Securock / DensDeck / DensDeck Prime min 0.25" | Optional: GAFGLAS #75, Tri-Ply #75 or #80 Ultima, mopped | 2+ GAFGLAS Ply 4 / Tri-Ply Ply 4 / FlexPly 6, mopped | GAFGLAS Mineral Surfaced, Tri-Ply BUR Granule or EnergyCap, mopped | -150 psf |
| A(2) | Structural concrete | EnergyGuard Polyiso or **Tapered Polyiso**, min 0.5" | Optional: **GAFGLAS Stratavent Perforated Venting Base Sheet, loose laid dry** | 2+ Ply 4 / FlexPly 6, mopped | Optional: same caps, mopped | -150 psf |

Application (A(1) and A(2)):
- Prime the existing asphaltic roof with **ASTM D41 or Matrix 307 primer at 0.75 gal/SQ**.
- Insulation in hot asphalt within the EVT range at **20-25 lb/SQ**, walked in (RAS 117).
- Top boards: 20-40 lb/SQ. Plies and cap: full mopping at 20-40 lb/SQ each.
- Surfacing is required on smooth membranes: gravel 400 lb/SQ or slag 300 lb/SQ in a 60 lb/SQ flood coat, or fibered aluminum coating.

## 25-0926.03 (Ruberoid mod-bit): what it covers
- **No system bonds insulation directly to the existing roof.** Insulated systems either mechanically fasten an anchor sheet through the existing roof and mop the insulation to it (A(1)-A(14)), or mechanically fasten the insulation (B, C, D).
- Concrete-type decks (A(1), A(2), B, C(1), D): **-45 psf** for all zones (GL #9). Plywood A(3)-A(14): -52.5 / -67.5 / -75 or -82.5 psf by fastening option (GL #7). Steel C(2): -60 psf.
- GAF requires either a dry-laid Stratavent perforated ply or a wood-fiber overlay on all polyiso in these systems.
- Non-insulated E(1), F(1), F(2): base sheet mopped to the existing roof, -45 psf.

## Takeoff notes
- **L4 preset match (provisional):** L4 is listed as "GAF Strat / Ply IV ×2 / Cap Sheet, hot mop." Read as Stratavent Perforated / GAFGLAS Ply 4 / GAFGLAS Mineral Surfaced Cap, that is **25-1017.03 System A(2)** when the existing roof is on a structural concrete deck. Confirm with Marc that "Strat" means Stratavent.
- **Wood deck:** A(3) or A(4). Budget anchor sheet + Drill-Tec fasteners and plates, and TAS 105 tests on the deck.
- **Add on every recover:** D41 primer at 0.75 gal/SQ, an extra mopping of asphalt for the insulation (20-25 lb/SQ), the 1521.10 bonded uplift verification (or 1521.11 if fastened), gravel removal down to a smooth or granule surface, and the RAS 111 edge and flashing package.
